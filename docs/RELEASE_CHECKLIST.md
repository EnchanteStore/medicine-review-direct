live card payments and delivery to a real patient stay off until the owner records the external
evidence in `launch_signoffs` and the screening rule set is clinically approved.

R-01, R-02 and R-03 in [DECISION_LOG.md](./DECISION_LOG.md) are built and are not launch
approvals. R-04 keeps the report PDF behind the signed-in session.
